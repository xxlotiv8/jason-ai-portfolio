Hand Gesture Control / 3D experiment

Make a little space.

Start with an empty space. Pinch with both hands to place two corners, then drag them into a 3D box. Point one finger up to pan; make a peace sign to rotate the view. Your camera view stays hidden.

HOW IT WORKS

Place. Shape. Move.

  1. 01
    Pinch two corners

    Hold a pinch with each hand to start a box.

  2. 02
    Drag to shape

    Move your hands to set the rectangle’s opposite corners; its depth follows the shorter side.

  3. 03
    Release to place

    Open either pinch. The box stays still where you leave it.

  4. 04
    Move the view

    Point one index finger up to pan, or hold a peace sign and move to rotate around the space.
